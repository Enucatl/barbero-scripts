That’s why Sultan Bayezid had said many years before: how foolish the king of Spain is to send me these subjects of his; they are the only people arriving here who can establish banking among us as well.

And so Doña Gracia keeps an eye on her nephews’ work while continuing to run the bank, build the family fortune, and protect Jews trying to escape the Christian world for the Ottoman Empire. She builds synagogues. For many years, one in Constantinople is known as the Lady’s Synagogue, and it becomes a gathering place for Portuguese Jews. She funds synagogues, Jewish hospitals, and rabbinical schools across the Ottoman Empire. She also tries to protect Jews who remain in the Christian world, though that’s getting harder and harder.
