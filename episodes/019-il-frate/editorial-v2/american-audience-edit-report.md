# Episode 019 American audience revision

Applied on 2026-10-02 to `script.en.md`, following the user's five-part instruction and request for a before-and-after report. The revision makes the narration self-contained for a contemporary American audience, removes the requested material, and preserves the trilogy premise and central character portrait.

The script changes from **8,398 to 7,315 spoken words**, a reduction of **1,083 words, approximately 13%**, and from **13 to 12 English chapters**. Counts exclude headings and editorial comments. Before-and-after entries below quote representative excerpts from every edited passage or identify a complete cut; ellipses indicate omitted surrounding text. The explanations describe editorial choices, not new historical findings.
