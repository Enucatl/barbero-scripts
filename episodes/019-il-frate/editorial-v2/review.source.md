## Findings

### SR-001 — Restore the Virgin as the subject who has witnessed the ordeal

- **Severity:** material. **Type:** routine translation repair; no new historical intervention.
- **Location:** CH-006, U-00512–U-00513; draft line445; Q-038/C-018.
- **Exact current snippet:** `For him, it really is the Virgin and child. He has seen everything. She has come to tell him that he has done right, that she is with him, and to reward him.`
- **Exact suggested repair:** `For him, it really is the Virgin and child. She has seen everything. She has come to tell him that he has done right, that she is with him, and to reward him.`
- **Evidence:** the settled Italian continues `la vergine col bambino che gli era apparsa, perché aveva visto tutto` into `e voleva dirgli che aveva fatto bene e che lei era con lui a ricompensarlo per questo`. The subject of both seeing and consoling is the Virgin. The present English switches the witnessing to Salimbene and loses the reason for her consolation. The repaired sentence remains inside the explicitly stated account of his belief, following the Freud aside; it asserts no externally verified apparition.
- **Disposition:** proposed repair; not applied by reviewer. Recheck this paragraph and its connection to the earlier father confrontation after repair.

### SR-002 — ED-002 overstates what the council evidence disproves

- **Severity:** material. **Type:** proposal rationale/scope; the current draft is faithful.
- **Location:** CH-003, U-00145–U-00152; draft line113; ED-002, C-007/Q-004/SRC-007.
- **Exact current draft target:** `The Church has never officially said, “Yes, that’s true.” Nor has it said, “It’s all nonsense.” People are left to make up their own minds.`
- **Exact proposed replacement under review:** `The Church has condemned one part of Joachim’s theology. But that doesn’t settle the question of these predictions. They still attract plenty of believers.`
- **Evidence:** immediately before the Italian claim, Barbero identifies the object as a system of biblical interpretation for predicting the future. The English preserves that antecedent. [Lateran IV, canon2](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum12-2.htm), independently opened for this review, condemns Joachim’s treatment of the Trinity. That document alone does not refute the proposition about the predictions. The dossier itself recognizes this distinction, but C-007 and the research review still describe the source as asserting unrestricted institutional silence. That is a disputable reading of its contextual scope, not a demonstrated factual error.
- **Exact recommended decision:** retain the existing draft target on the evidence currently supplied; change ED-002’s recommendation to `retain`, leaving the user decision `pending`. If the coordinator keeps the contextual alternative available, use this exact rationale: `Optional context: Lateran IV condemned a Trinitarian treatise by Joachim. This does not by itself disprove the lecture’s narrower claim about his predictive system. The replacement adds that distinction and removes the lecture’s statement about freedom to form an opinion; it requires an editorial choice.` Do not present this as a compulsory correction. No additional script replacement is requested.
- **Disposition:** proposal challenge awaiting coordinator disposition. Further evidence about institutional responses to the relevant prophetic system could justify revisiting it; research status alone cannot.

### SR-003 — ED-008 should distinguish the pike’s sender from its presenter

- **Severity:** material. **Type:** precision repair to an unapproved historical proposal.
- **Location:** CH-010, U-00687–U-00691; ED-008 proposed text, decisions.yaml around line400; Q-061/C-025/SRC-004.
- **Exact current draft snippet:** `On one occasion they are coming out of Sens to meet the king. As the king arrives, the bishop of Sens sends him a present.`
- **Exact proposed snippet under review:** `On one occasion they are coming out of Sens to meet the king. As the king arrives, the treasurer of the church of Sens presents him with a gift.`
- **Exact suggested replacement within ED-008:** `On one occasion they are coming out of Sens to meet the king. As the king arrives, a gift comes for him from the treasurer of the church of Sens.`
- **Evidence:** independently inspected [MGH p222](https://www.dmgh.de/mgh_ss_32/img/300/mgh_ss_32_00253.jpg), lines33–36, says the gift is presented on behalf of the church treasurer. [MGH p223](https://www.dmgh.de/mgh_ss_32/img/300/mgh_ss_32_00254.jpg), opening lines, distinguishes the sender and the presenter when describing the king’s thanks. The new proposal should preserve that distinction. Its stated aim is to identify the gift’s source without assuming a bishop commissioned it; the suggested wording accomplishes exactly that.
- **Disposition:** proposed repair to ED-008, not permission to apply ED-008. No change to the faithful draft is authorized by this finding. The rest of the consolidated proposal remains sound within its declared scope.
