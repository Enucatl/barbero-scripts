The federal garrison continues to hold the fort and does not hand it over. On 12 April, the authorities of South Carolina have fire opened on Fort Sumter. The following day the garrison surrenders, but in the meantime Lincoln has already asked for full powers to recruit an army and initially retake Fort Sumter. After Fort Sumter’s surrender is agreed on 13 April and the garrison is evacuated on 14 April, Lincoln’s 15 April proclamation calls for 75,000 militia volunteers for ninety days. [N-031] And so, and so the American Civil War really begins, after people have been talking about it for thirty years. Lincoln will be shot on 14 April 1865, four years and one day after Fort Sumter’s surrender agreement; Lee has surrendered, but major Confederate forces elsewhere will surrender in the following weeks and months. [N-032] Slavery has been abolished throughout the United States. The Thirteenth Amendment abolishes slavery without compensation to enslavers; citizenship and equal protection are established through Reconstruction, especially the Fourteenth Amendment, amid sustained white resistance. [N-033] An enormous social advance achieved without anyone having really thought about it, without anyone seriously wanting it until shortly before, and in the midst of a white population that is not at all prepared for this outcome and that, by a large majority, does not want equality either in the South or in the North. And the consequences are still felt today. Thank you.
