### SF-001 — material — remaining universal reassurance in CH-004

**Location:** CH-004, U-00328–00329; final paragraph. Related ED-001, ED-004, ED-010 and ED-013; C-009–010, C-014–016.

**Evidence:** The draft faithfully renders the lecture's “non ci pensavan più, si eran messi il cuore in pace” as “We've just seen that by 999, people have put their minds at rest.” But the three documents demonstrate plans for the future by particular actors. They do not establish that people generally had ceased worrying. ED-010/013 explicitly acknowledge the spread of apocalyptic ideas; applying them leaves this earlier claim unreconciled. This is inherited source overreach, not a mistranslation, and needs an editorial decision.

**Exact proposed new target:**

> We've just seen that by 999, people have put their minds at rest.

**Exact proposed replacement:**

> We've just seen people making plans for the future in 999.

Add this to the same human choice about limiting the inference from documents and clerical rebuttal. The surrounding question about earlier predictions remains intact. This does not claim that all anxiety is disproved. The Tortona conclusion is also an inference about particular contracting parties; it can remain as Barbero's interpretation, but should not be described in verification as a documentary statement of their private beliefs.

### SF-002 — material — ED-014 implies an established right to preach

**Evidence:** The reproduced Latin explicitly says the annalist regarded the preaching ministry as something Thiota had seized contrary to ecclesiastical custom. “Strip her of the right to preach” can imply she previously possessed an acknowledged right. The proposal otherwise handles all three same-year references and the clerical followers well.

**Exact change within ED-014's replacement:**

> They have her publicly flogged and strip her of the right to preach.

to:

> They have her publicly flogged and forbid her to preach.

Retain the subsequent explicitly dramatized dismissal and the annalist-attributed ending. This is a refinement of pending wording, not permission to apply ED-014.

### SF-004 — material — ED-017 leaves its transmission qualification inside a confident Renaissance frame

The documentary limitation concerns attribution and transmission, not merely which individual humanist held the belief. This is a proposed-treatment problem; it does not establish a 1690 forgery.

**Exact proposed scope change:** extend ED-017's current target backward to include the immediately preceding sentence and marker:

```text
Then, at last, comes the Renaissance.

```

**Exact beginning of the enlarged proposed replacement:**

```text
<!-- Q-033; C-020 -->

Take the annals transmitted under the name of Trithemius. Most of us, quite reasonably, have never heard of him, but he’s an important German humanist of the early sixteenth century.

```

Follow this with ED-017's existing second and third paragraphs unchanged, beginning “These annals repeat the prodigies…” and “That last part isn’t in Sigebert…”. This expansion does not overlap ED-016 or ED-029; preserve the moved marker and recalculate the target hash.
