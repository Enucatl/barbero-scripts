# Episode 076 outline

## Overview

Barbero traces the French monarchy's recurring fiscal crises from Louis XIV through the opening
of the Revolution. The lecture follows successive attempts to escape debt through taxation,
paper money, commercial monopolies, liberal reform, lotteries, borrowing, and representative
consent, while emphasizing the political interests that defeated or distorted each remedy.
