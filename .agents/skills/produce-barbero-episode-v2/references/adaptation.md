# One coherent adaptation

Read the complete Italian script, brief, evidence review, and relevant source records before
writing. Use one Astra writer for `TRIAL/script.draft.en.md`. Chapters may be saved incrementally
for practical output limits, but retain the whole episode's context and check joins. Verify that
all required input text was actually read; a model's nominal context capacity is not proof of this.

## Draft contract

Write for an intelligent contemporary American without specialist knowledge of the period.
Produce natural spoken English directly: clear subjects, audible causal links, idiomatic verbs,
contractions where natural, and sentences that can be understood on first hearing. Recast Italian
syntax freely while retaining meaning, order, uncertainty, emphasis, substantive examples, and
the function of repetition. Preserve dialogue and the setup/payoff of jokes. Do not turn dramatic
exchanges into summaries merely to shorten the script.

Keep useful historical terms; contextualize or briefly gloss them when needed. Prefer familiar
words where precision survives. Do not replace specific meaning with generic simplification.
Use existing context to clarify a referent. A gloss that introduces a new historical assertion
requires evidence and a proposal. Avoid generic podcast hooks, manufactured suspense, added jokes,
and mechanical callbacks. Keep meaningful digressions unless the user approves their removal.

Use historical present for the active narrative scene. Allow past/perfect forms for events already
completed relative to that scene and ordinary retrospective statements where they clarify time.
Use future or conditional forms according to chronology and uncertainty. Do not force historical
present into every background fact, publication date, or quotation. Exact source quotations retain
their wording and tense. A stricter user-specified tense policy overrides this default.

Adapt lecture-relative wording such as references to previous/next lectures to the podcast format
when its meaning is unambiguous; retain historical yesterday/tomorrow. Removal of substantive live
framing, entire examples, first-use identities, or dialogue belongs in the decision queue. Remove
pronunciation spellings from narration; preserve substantive parenthetical asides.

Use the same numbered chapters and exact coverage comments as `script.it.md`. Keep stable research
IDs in comments adjacent to relevant passages; do not invent claim/source IDs or place production
metadata in spoken text. A proposed cut does not erase the corresponding provenance range. The
English H1 may begin with the existing approved audience title or a faithful translation of the
source title; a new audience title is an explicit proposal.

## Research and editorial departures

Integrate corrections already approved for this episode naturally in the narrative voice. Preserve
the approved meaning and record the decision provenance in the brief. Do not insert fact-checking
asides explaining what Barbero got wrong unless the user specifically requests that presentation.

For new factual corrections, recovered quotation substitutions, substantive cuts/additions, or
title choices, keep the draft faithful to the source and prepare exact alternatives in
`decisions.yaml`. A source claim that research disputes can remain in the review draft while its
correction awaits approval; it must not be represented as an approved recording script. Surface
unresolved material findings before completion. Never put an agent's recommendation into the
human decision field.

Contextually translate Barbero's quotations without claiming recovered verbatim authority. An
English contextual rendering may naturally coincide with a researched original; do not change
correct wording to force a difference. Preserve quotation/commentary alternation. In a replacement
proposal, give exact protected source spans and use only relevant clauses; distinguish quoted
words from paraphrase and disclose composite boundaries.

Do not produce a full faithful-English baseline just to compare against it. The Italian and the
explicit departures provide that audit trail. If a difficult passage needs a literal rendering
for diagnosis, keep it as a bounded note in its review or proposal.

## Before fresh review

Check every chapter against the Italian for omissions, changes to modality, compressed causal
links, uncertain identities, and repeated translation calques. Read the whole English as narration.
Fix objective language defects in this draft. Do not initiate another routine rewrite of every
chapter. Save a complete draft and concrete material proposals for the independent reviewers.

Record the writer model/effort and input hashes in the review package. Use evidence and review
outcomes to evaluate this workflow; a fluent draft or a structurally valid file does not establish
editorial quality.
