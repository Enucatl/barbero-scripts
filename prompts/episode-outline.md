# Episode structural outline

Read `{transcript_path}` in full and replace `{outline_path}` with a structural outline of the
lecture. Work from the corrected transcript only; do not perform external research in this pass.

## Purpose

Map the lecture accurately before research or English adaptation. Preserve Barbero's sequence and
distinguish his main causal argument from examples, analogies, asides, and audience-facing remarks.

## Required structure

For every substantive section include:

- A numbered, descriptive heading.
- Inclusive utterance-ID range.
- Original-audio timestamp range from the transcript headings.
- A concise account of the section's argument.
- Supporting examples and narrative steps.
- Digressions or modern analogies, when present.
- Candidate quotations and attributed statements needing verification.
- Candidate central claims needing research.

Add an opening overview of the lecture's thesis and narrative arc. End with consolidated lists of
candidate quotation IDs, claim IDs, recurring terminology, and issues that
cannot be determined from the transcript alone.

Use provisional stable identifiers `Q-001`, `Q-002`, ... and `C-001`, `C-002`, ... in order of
first appearance. Do not use `SRC` identifiers because sources have not yet been researched.

## Editorial boundary

- Do not fact-check, introduce external facts, or silently correct Barbero's interpretation.
- Do not translate or draft English narration.
- Do not omit a lecture passage merely because it seems incidental.
- Prefer transcript references over copied transcript text.
- Quote the transcript only when needed to identify a research target.
- Mark uncertainty explicitly rather than inventing structure or attribution.

## Verification

Before finishing:

1. Confirm the entire transcript is covered in order with no unintended range gaps.
2. Confirm every referenced utterance ID exists.
3. Confirm timestamp ranges agree with the transcript headings.
4. Confirm provisional `Q` and `C` identifiers are unique and sequential.
5. Report section, quotation-target, claim-target, and unresolved-issue counts.
